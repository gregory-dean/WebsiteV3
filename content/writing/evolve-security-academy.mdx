## The short version

I completed Evolve Security Academy's immersive cybersecurity apprenticeship and earned the ESCP certification. The program was built to feel like operations pressure, not slide decks — threat hunting, red teaming, malware analysis, and live incident response scenarios.

## What changed in how I work

- **Think in paths, not tools.** Attackers chain misconfigurations, weak controls, and human factors. Mapping those paths (often with MITRE ATT&CK) matters more than memorizing product UIs.
- **Offense sharpens defense.** Running the exploit makes the detection gap obvious. That loop is now how I approach lab work.
- **Write for the team that has to live with the finding.** Clear remediation beats clever severity scores nobody can act on.

## Where I'm headed

I'm looking for SOC Analyst I or Junior Penetration Tester roles — places where I can keep practicing under experienced mentors while contributing real coverage. The homelab and public writeups are how I keep that muscle warm between interviews and engagements.
